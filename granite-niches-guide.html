SECTION 1

What a Granite Niche Is

A permanent, above-ground place for cremated remains

Granite fronts are outside. All three places in this guide stand in the open air: a pavilioned courtyard, a garden wall, a terrace path. A glass-front niche, by contrast, is always inside a building.

A niche is a compartment for cremated remains inside a permanent memorial structure. The urn goes in and the niche is closed with a stone front. That front is what you and your family see and touch when you visit.

“Granite front” means the closure is stone. The contents aren't visible. The name, dates and any inscription are engraved into the granite or carried on a bronze plate. The wall reads as one uniform stone face rather than a row of individual displays.

Two rights of interment come with the niche at all three places here, which is two people, usually a husband and wife, on one contract. It's a permanent address, cared for by the cemetery, with no ground burial and no grave to open.

If you want the urn to be visible, that's a glass front, at the Mountain View and Eternal Light columbariums. Neither is covered here. See the Glass-Front Niche Guide, or the live maps (Mountain View · Eternal Light).

SECTION 2

Rock of Ages Columbarium

A granite courtyard, seven double-sided walls, two rights in every niche

Rock of Ages is a granite columbarium built around an open courtyard, sheltered by a timber pavilion. Seven double-sided sections carry five tiers of five niches on each face: walls A, B and C on the south bank, E, F and G on the north, and Wall D standing across the west head. Faces that look into the courtyard are the interior faces; the rest look out over the lawn. Two granite memorial benches sit in the courtyard.

Rock of Ages niche

Depending on the wall and the tier. Interior courtyard faces and the higher tiers price above the outward-facing walls and the lower tiers; the least expensive niches on the site are the bottom tier of Wall G’s outside face, the most expensive the top tiers of the interior faces.

Two rights of interment come with the niche, so two people may be placed in it. That is the standard here; there is no separate companion product to buy.

SECTION 3

Garden of Meditation Niches

A stepped black granite garden wall, sold as companions

The Garden of Meditation niches are a stepped wall of polished black granite set into a planted garden, with bronze plates and bronze vase holders on the faces. The wall steps up from low wings at either end to a taller centre section. Its official property address is GOM-1-1-ROW-SPACE, so a specific niche reads as, for example, GOM-1-1-C-7.

Garden of Meditation niche

Companion niche · two inurnment rights · one niche vase included
GENERALLY
$5,995–$8,995

Depending on the row: the lower rows are the least expensive and the top of the centre section the most. Only part of the wall is offered at any one time. Ask us to confirm today’s availability, and anything not currently priced is confirmed before it is quoted.

Sold as a companion niche (2 inurnment rights). Due to niche size, the Interlude Urn is required, and two fit per niche. The companion capacity and the urn requirement are one fact, not two: the niche holds two people because two Interlude Urns fit in it. The Interlude Urn is $665 each, plus 10.4% sales tax, merchandise quoted alongside the niche rather than as a fee. One niche vase is included.

SECTION 7

Talking It Over

What to decide, and what we will confirm for you
  • Decide whether you want a granite front or a glass front. Whether the urn should be visible is usually the first thing families settle.
  • Decide whether you are placing one person or two. Two rights come with the niche at all three locations, so a couple is one contract.
  • At the Garden of Meditation, the Interlude Urn is required. Bring that into the urn conversation early rather than choosing an urn first.
  • Walk the three locations if you can. They are very different places, and families almost always know which one is theirs once they have stood in it.
  • Ask us to confirm the exact space, its price and today’s availability before anything is signed.
