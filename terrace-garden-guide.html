SECTION 1

The Path

A planted terrace between the mausoleum wings

The Terrace Garden Memorial Path is the newest cremation property at Washington Memorial Park. A paved terrace runs between the mausoleum wings, with a bluestone and river-rock circle at its centre, planted beds either side, and a freestanding black granite niche bank standing across the far end.A bluestone and river-rock circle sits at the centre of the terrace, with planted beds either side of the walk and the niche bank across the far end.

There are two quite different things for sale here. The niche bank is a wall of 40 granite-front niches, priced by row. Along the path stand 9 individual memorials (cremation posts, pedestal benches, a companion columbarium and a birdbath), each of which holds cremated remains in its own right.

All of it is new. Nothing on the path has been sold and re-offered. This garden opened with everything in it available.

The bank is eight niches across and five rows high, and it's priced by row: the middle row is the highest, the top and bottom rows the lowest. Niches are addressed TGN-C-4 and so on.

SECTION 3ALONG THE PATH

Along the Path

Nine individual memorials, from a single post to a companion columbarium

Cremation posts

Granite post · one or two rights

The smallest memorials on the path, set into the bark beds either side of the walk. Single posts hold one; a double post holds two, and some carry a carved rose, daffodils or birds.

Posts $8,000–$16,000

Benches, columbarium and birdbath

Granite · two to four rights

The larger pieces: two pedestal benches, a companion columbarium with an alcove, and a birdbath on a pedestal. Each holds cremated remains inside it.

Larger memorials $24,000–$52,000

The 9 memorials along the path carry 1–4 rights of interment, depending on the piece, and run $8,000–$52,000 across the whole set. Ask us for the item-by-item list if you want to compare them side by side.

SECTION 5WHAT YOU WILL ACTUALLY SEE

What You Will See Today

The garden as built, not as drawn

The beds are dark bark mulch. The marketing artwork for the Terrace Garden draws them as bright green turf; the built garden has bark around the posts, which is what every photograph in this guide shows and what you'll find when you walk it. I'd rather you saw it here than be surprised by it out there.
