SECTION 1

What Is a Columbarium?

A dignified above-ground resting place for cremated remains

A columbarium is a structure with individual compartments — called niches — designed to hold urns containing cremated remains. Columbariums offer a permanent, secure, and elegant alternative to scattering or in-ground burial.

Placement carries charges beyond the niche or garden space itself. The Cemetery Property Guide walks through the space, endowment care, opening and closing, and the rest.

Each niche is sealed with a granite or bronze faceplate that can be engraved with the name, dates, and a personal inscription. Niches are available as single (one urn) or companion (two urns side by side).

Washington Memorial Park features five columbarium locations, each with its own setting and character. Families are welcome to visit any time during park hours.

SECTION 3

Urn Garden Burial

In-ground burial of urns in a dedicated garden setting

For families who prefer an in-ground option, the Rose Urn Garden in Section 18 provides a landscaped setting for urn burial. The urn is placed in a smaller grave space and covered with a flush granite marker, similar to a traditional burial but in a compact, garden-like area.

Urn garden spaces accommodate one or two urns per space. An urn vault is required to protect the urn underground. The space is marked with a flush granite marker that can be personalized with names, dates, and an epitaph.

URN GARDEN INCLUDES
  • Garden space — dedicated plot in Section 18
  • Endowment Care — perpetual grounds maintenance
  • Opening & closing — interment service by park staff
  • Recording fee — permanent record of placement
  • Urn vault required — protects the urn underground
  • Flush granite marker — quoted separately (see Marker Guide)
