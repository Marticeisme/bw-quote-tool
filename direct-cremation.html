What Is Direct Cremation?

A dignified, straightforward option for families

Direct cremation — sometimes called simple cremation — is a dignified, straightforward option that focuses on the essential: caring for your loved one with respect and professionalism, without the additional services of a viewing or funeral ceremony.

With direct cremation, your loved one is transferred to our care, prepared for cremation, and cremated in a timely manner. The cremated remains are then returned to the family in a temporary container or the urn of your choosing.

Many families who choose direct cremation still hold a celebration of life, memorial service, or private gathering at a later date — on their own terms and timeline.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
