## Conventions carried over from MVC/ROAC (read their builders; these are settled law)

- 3D CSS courtyard/island with orbit + zoom clamps, face-on presets that exactly fit each
  face, flat per-wall tabs, overview, print = static flat grids (no JS in print output).
- Tap detector from the pointer stream (native clicks after gestures are swallowed);
  hover frozen mid-drag; hover/detail cards pointer-transparent; card opens BESIDE the
  niche (bottom sheet on phones); pinned selection mirrored into flat tabs; print follows
  the highlight (selection > wall tab > everything) and a highlighted niche prints its
  full pricing card.
- **Status is pattern-coded, never hue-coded**: SOLD = blacked-out (as ROAC's Occupied),
  white badges. **No price rendered anywhere for a sold niche** (data keeps the price,
  gates prove non-rendering).
- Exact prices on chips ($18,695, not $18.7K). Detail card math: price + 10% ECF +
  Open & Closing $835 + Recording $225 (mirror how MVC presents ECF+O&C; Bronze Scroll and
  Vase are optional add-ons — list them in the page footer/legend, not in per-niche math).
- "← Quote Tool" back button in the header (maps open in new tabs with no history).
- Header notes geometry is estimated from photographs.
