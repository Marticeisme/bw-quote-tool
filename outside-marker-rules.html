Outside Marker Installation
Rules & Pricing

What it costs, what sizes are allowed, and how the approval works if you buy your marker somewhere else.
WASHINGTON MEMORIAL PARK · SEATAC, WA
Overview

Before You Begin

Read this first

You’re welcome to buy a marker from another provider. I want you to know upfront that it costs more to do it that way. Our own markers are made for this park and the price includes everything; an outside marker adds fees for the review, the paperwork and the installation, and those are set out in full below.

Whoever makes the marker, we install it. Outside vendors can’t do installations on the grounds here, so your vendor delivers the marker and our installer sets it.

Pricing

3rd Party Installation Fees

There are three fees on every outside marker, and one more if there is already a marker on the space. The installation fee covers the foundation and setting the stone. The inspection and design review is half the installation fee, and it pays for someone to check your vendor’s proof against what the park allows before the marker is cut, because a marker that turns up the wrong size can’t be installed and can’t be returned. The $250 administrative fee covers the paperwork and coordinating with your vendor. We don’t discount these, and they are paid in full before any work starts.

Generally$250–$1,600

Where a particular one falls in that range depends on the size, the location and the options you choose. I would rather give you a real number than a guess. Call or email me and I will put an exact quote in writing for you, with no obligation.

Requirements

Key Rules & Requirements

  • 1
    The property (plot) the marker will be installed on must be paid in full. For companion markers that span two plots, both plots must be paid in full.
  • 2
    All memorial proofs are subject to approval based on cemetery standards and size guidelines. Proofs may be rejected and require redesign if the size, design, or placement does not conform to park requirements.
  • 3
    The memorial must be ordered by the rightful plot owner. If someone other than the owner is ordering, the owner must sign a Waiver of Rights or co-sign the contract.
  • 4
    We install every marker, using our approved installer. Outside vendors can’t install on cemetery grounds.
  • 5
    Nothing is scheduled for installation until the paperwork is signed, the proof is approved and the fees are paid in full.
Size Guide

Allowed Marker Sizes

The following marker sizes and types are permitted at Washington Memorial Park. Your memorial proof must conform to one of these sizes. All dimensions are in inches (width × depth).Your proof must match one of these. Dimensions are inches, width × depth.

Single, one interment

One grave space · one interment right
  • 28×16 Single marker
  • 32×20 Single marker
  • 24×12 Govt. or Govt. match w/ foundation to 28×16 (24×12 standard in Vets sections)
  • 28×34 Half ledger, vertical orientation
  • 36×72 Full ledger

Single, two interments

One grave space · two interment rights
  • 28×16 Companion marker, or single at head of grave
  • 32×20 Companion marker
  • 28×34 Half ledger companion, vertical
  • 36×72 Full ledger companion

Companion, two graves

Spans two plots · both must be paid in full
  • 44×20 Companion
  • 60×20 Companion
  • 36×72 Full ledger companion

Special Sections

Lake Urn Garden · Infants · Uprights · Benches
  • 15×10 Lake Urn Garden, singles only (except pre-placed Williamsburg companion units)
  • 18×10 Infant sections. Garden of Verses is granite only
  • 36″ max Upright foundations (Sec. 6, Sec. 23 blocks, Cascade Court only). Consult superintendent for Sec. 23.
  • 36×18 Bench companion (Sec. 6, 23, blocks of 20, Rose Urn Garden Sec. 18)
  • 44×20 Bench companion
