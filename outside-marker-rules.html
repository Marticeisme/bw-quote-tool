Outside Marker Installation
Rules & Pricing

Fees, allowed marker sizes, and the approval process for all 3rd party memorial installations at Washington Memorial Park.
WASHINGTON MEMORIAL PARK · SEATAC, WA
Overview

Before You Begin

Important

Bonney Watson memorialization products are designed specifically for the park and remain the preferred option for ease of process, cost efficiency, and long-term consistency. Families may choose to use an outside vendor; however, additional coordination, compliance, and processing fees will apply.

All memorial installations within the park must be performed by Bonney Watson’s approved installation provider. Outside vendors are not permitted to perform installations on cemetery grounds.

Pricing

3rd Party Installation Fees

All outside memorialization products are subject to the following fees. No discounts are applied to 3rd party memorialization fees. Full payment is required before any work begins.

Process

How It Works

Your Family Service Director will guide you through each step. Here’s an overview of what to expect.

1

Submit Proof

Provide the memorial design proof from your outside vendor to your Family Service Director.

2

Review & Approval

The proof is reviewed for compliance with cemetery size and design standards. Revisions may be required.

3

Payment & Paperwork

Sign the Hold Harmless Agreement, cemetery contract, and submit full payment for all applicable fees.

4

Installation

Bonney Watson coordinates delivery and installation. You’ll be notified once the marker is placed.

Requirements

Key Rules & Requirements

  • 1
    The property (plot) the marker will be installed on must be paid in full. For companion markers that span two plots, both plots must be paid in full.
  • 2
    All memorial proofs are subject to approval based on cemetery standards and size guidelines. Proofs may be rejected and require redesign if the size, design, or placement does not conform to park requirements.
  • 3
    The memorial must be ordered by the rightful plot owner. If someone other than the owner is ordering, the owner must sign a Waiver of Rights or co-sign the contract.
  • 4
    All installations are performed exclusively by Bonney Watson’s approved installation provider. Outside vendors may not perform installations on cemetery grounds.
  • 5
    All required documentation, approvals, and full payment must be received before the memorial will be scheduled for installation.
Size Guide

Allowed Marker Sizes

The following marker sizes and types are permitted at Washington Memorial Park. Your memorial proof must conform to one of these sizes. All dimensions are in inches (width × depth).

Single — One Interment

One grave space · one interment right
  • 28×16 Single marker
  • 32×20 Single marker
  • 24×12 Govt. or Govt. match w/ foundation to 28×16 (24×12 standard in Vets sections)
  • 28×34 Half ledger, vertical orientation
  • 36×72 Full ledger

Single — Two Interments

One grave space · two interment rights
  • 28×16 Companion marker, or single at head of grave
  • 32×20 Companion marker
  • 28×34 Half ledger companion, vertical
  • 36×72 Full ledger companion

Companion — Two Graves

Spans two plots · both must be paid in full
  • 44×20 Companion
  • 60×20 Companion
  • 36×72 Full ledger companion

Special Sections

Lake Urn Garden · Infants · Uprights · Benches
  • 15×10 Lake Urn Garden — singles only (except pre-placed Williamsburg companion units)
  • 18×10 Infant sections — Garden of Verses is granite only
  • 36″ max Upright foundations (Sec. 6, Sec. 23 blocks, Cascade Court only). Consult superintendent for Sec. 23.
  • 36×18 Bench companion (Sec. 6, 23, blocks of 20, Rose Urn Garden Sec. 18)
  • 44×20 Bench companion

Not Sure Which Size?

Contact your Family Service Director before ordering from your vendor. We’ll confirm the exact size allowed for your specific plot and section.

Questions? We’re Here to Help.

If you’re considering an outside vendor, reach out before you order — we can confirm the allowed sizes for your plot and walk you through the process.
