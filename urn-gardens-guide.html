Urn Gardens

The Lake Urn Garden and the Rose Urn Garden at Washington Memorial Park — two memorial gardens set aside for cremated remains, and the ways a family can be placed in them.
SECTION 1

What an Urn Garden Is

A garden designed for urns, not for graves

An urn garden is a type of memorial garden that is specifically designed to accommodate the placement of urns containing the cremated remains of loved ones. The ground, the planting and the memorials are all scaled to an urn rather than a casket, so the setting is smaller and more garden-like than a lawn section.

Washington Memorial Park has two: the Lake Urn Garden, on the shore of the lake at the south end of the park, and the Rose Urn Garden, in the Garden of Flowers.

1 or 2 rights, by space
A right of interment is permission to place one person. Rose Urn Garden spaces carry two rights, so one space can hold a couple on one contract. Lake Urn Garden burial spaces carry one right each, and some of the cremation posts carry two. We confirm the number of rights on the specific space before anything is written down.
SECTION 2

The Two Gardens

The same kind of property in two very different settings

Lake Urn Garden

On the lake shore · ground spaces, boulders and cremation posts
GROUND SPACE
$5,495

The lake, its two fountains and the magnolias above it are the setting. Alongside the ground spaces this is the only garden in the park offering urns placed in boulders near the water and cremation posts. Endowment care of $825 is added to the space price.

Rose Urn Garden

In the Garden of Flowers · ground spaces in a planted setting
GROUND SPACE
$4,395

A planted garden rather than a waterside one, laid out in rings with benches around it. It is the quieter of the two and the one families choose when they want the garden itself, not the view. The price is the same inside or outside the rose perimeter. Endowment care of $660 is added to the space price.

Generally$4,395–$5,495

Where a particular one falls in that range depends on the size, the location and the options you choose. I would rather give you a real number than a guess — call or email me and I will put an exact quote in writing for you, with no obligation.

SECTION 3

Three Ways to Be Placed

Options for urns to be placed in boulders near the lake as well as cremation posts

In the ground

The urn is placed in a garden space and marked with a bronze memorial set flush with the lawn, exactly as a grave would be. Available in both gardens.

Opening & closing $875

In a boulder

The urn goes inside a natural boulder set near the water at the Lake Urn Garden. The boulder is closed with a bronze cap carrying the inscription.

Opening & closing $1,425

In a cremation post

A low granite post standing in the garden bed, holding the urn above ground with the bronze memorial on its face. Also called a Cremorial unit.

Memorial from $5,670
