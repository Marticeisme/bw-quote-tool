SECTION 1

What an Urn Garden Is

A garden designed for urns, not for graves

An urn garden is a garden built for urns instead of caskets. The urn is buried in the ground and marked the same way any other grave would be. Everything is scaled to an urn rather than a casket, so the spaces, the planting and the memorials are smaller, and it feels more like a garden than a lawn section.

Washington Memorial Park has two: the Lake Urn Garden, on the shore of the lake at the south end of the park, and the Rose Urn Garden, in the Garden of Flowers.

1 or 2 rights, by space
A right of interment is permission to place one person. Rose Urn Garden spaces carry two, so one space can hold a couple on one contract. Lake Urn Garden burial spaces carry one, and some cremation posts carry two.A right of interment is permission to place one person. Rose Urn Garden spaces carry two rights, so one space can hold a couple on one contract. Lake Urn Garden burial spaces carry one right each, and some of the cremation posts carry two. We confirm the number of rights on the specific space before anything is written down.

SECTION 3Three ways to be placed

Three Ways to Be Placed

Options for urns to be placed in boulders near the lake as well as cremation posts

In the ground. The urn is placed in a garden space and marked with a bronze memorial set flush with the lawn, exactly as a grave would be. Available in both gardens. Opening and closing $875.

In a boulder. The urn goes inside a natural boulder set near the water at the Lake Urn Garden, closed with a bronze cap carrying the inscription. Opening and closing $1,425.

In a cremation post. A low granite post standing in the garden bed, holding the urn above ground with the bronze memorial on its face. Memorial from $5,670.
