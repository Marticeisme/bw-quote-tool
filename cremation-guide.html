Understanding Your Cremation Options

Plans, pricing, and what’s included, explained clearly.

There is no single right answer. We have four main cremation plans, and each one includes our professional services, bringing them into our care, and the cremation itself. The difference is how much ceremony you want. If you are still weighing cremation against burial, the Cremation or Burial guide sets the two side by side.

Each plan includes the professional services of our funeral directors, the transfer of your loved one to our care, and the cremation itself. You can add a viewing, a formal ceremony, or simply choose a quiet, direct cremation.

The four main paths are a Viewing & Funeral Plan, a Memorial Service Plan, a Basic Cremation with Identification Viewing, and Direct Cremation, the most affordable option, starting at $3,055.

Key Things to Know
  • All plans require a cremation container purchased from Bonney Watson to qualify for the bundled price.
  • Friday, Saturday, Sunday, holiday, and evening ceremonies may carry additional fees.
  • An urn allowance is included in most plans; see the urn selection in Sections 7–9.
  • Direct cremation starts at $3,055 and is the most affordable option.

Section 3

Information About Death Certificates

How many to order, how to get them, and what they cost.

A death certificate is an official legal document you’ll need to settle accounts, transfer property, claim benefits, and handle other affairs after a loss.

How the process works. A physician signs and states the cause of death. Our team gathers the required information and files the completed certificate with the county, typically 7–10 business days.

How many do you need. We recommend one certified copy for each of: life insurance, bank accounts, real estate or mortgage, retirement or pension, military benefits, vehicle titles, and utilities or credit cards.

Cost in Washington. Each certified copy is $25.00. Pierce County adds a $4.00 shipping fee plus a $10.00 review fee. If a correction is needed, the WA Department of Health allows one free exchange, but corrected certificates can take up to 8 weeks.

Key Things to Know
  • Order more than you think you need. Reordering later takes time.
  • We handle the filing on your behalf, so there is no need to visit the health department yourself.
  • Ask each institution whether they require a certified copy or accept a photocopy.

Cremation Containers

The enclosure used before and during cremation, available in several styles and prices.

By federal law, every funeral home offering cremation must make an alternative container available. No law requires a traditional casket. The container is used before and during cremation; an urn is what you select afterward. Containers range from $480 to $1,570, and the material has no effect on the quality of the cremation.
