SECTION 1

The Columbarium

Granite, open to the sky, under a timber pavilion

Rock of Ages is a granite columbarium built around an open courtyard. Seven double-sided sections stand under a timber pavilion: walls A, B and C along the south bank, E, F and G along the north, and Wall D standing across the west head of the courtyard. Every section carries five tiers of five niches on each of its two faces.

A niche here is a compartment for cremated remains, closed with a granite front. The urn goes inside; the front is what your family sees and touches when they visit, and the name and dates are engraved into the stone itself.A niche is a compartment for cremated remains, closed with a granite front. The urn goes inside. The front is what your family sees and touches when they visit, and the name and dates are engraved into the stone itself rather than carried on a plate.

Every niche carries 2 rights of interment, which is two people, usually a husband and wife, on one contract. There's no separate companion product to buy here. Two is simply what a Rock of Ages niche holds.

ROCK OF AGES IN SHORT
  • Seven double-sided granite sections, 350 niches in all, under one open pavilion.
  • Two rights of interment in every niche.
  • Granite front. The urn is not visible; the name is engraved in the stone.
  • Outdoors, with lawn on every side and two granite memorial benches in the courtyard.
SECTION 2THE TWO SIDES

Inside and Out

Courtyard faces and outward faces are two different places to be

Half of the faces look into the courtyard and half look out over the lawn. The courtyard faces are the sheltered, enclosed side. You stand between two walls with the benches beside you. The outward faces look across open grass and trees. It's the same building and the same stone, but it's a genuinely different place to stand. Most people know which one is theirs once they've stood in both.

Tiers run A at the bottom to E at the top, with five spaces across each face numbered 1 to 5. Price follows the tier and the face: the higher tiers and the courtyard faces are the most sought after, the lower tiers on the outward faces the least.Tiers run A at the bottom to E at the top, five spaces across each face. Price follows the tier and the face: the higher tiers and the courtyard faces are the most sought after, the lower tiers on the outward faces the least.

Two granite memorial benches sit in the courtyard. Both have been sold. They are not available for purchase and cannot be quoted; they are part of the place rather than something to buy.

SECTION 3WHAT IT COSTS

Choosing a Niche

The two decisions that set the price

A courtyard-facing niche

Granite front · two rights · the sheltered side

These face into the courtyard, between the two banks, with the memorial benches in front of them. They are the most sought-after faces here and are priced above the outward ones.

Courtyard faces, generally $8,795–$15,995

An outward-facing niche

Granite front · two rights · looking over the lawn

The outward faces look out across mown lawn and trees rather than into the courtyard. The least expensive niches at Rock of Ages are the bottom tier of an outward face.

Outward faces, generally $7,995–$13,195

Across the whole columbarium, niches currently for sale run $7,995–$15,995, depending on the face and the tier. I'll confirm the exact price of any niche before anything is written down.

SECTION 4WHAT ELSE YOU PAY FOR

What Comes With It

What the niche price covers, and what is charged alongside it

The price of the niche buys the space and its two rights of interment. The engraving, the opening and closing when someone is placed, the recording of the interment, the bronze vase and the Endowment Care Fund contribution are charged alongside it.

Additional charges

Opening and closing, recording, endowment care and any inscription are charged in addition to the price of the space. They are straightforward, and they differ by location and by what you choose. Call or email me and I will put the exact figures in writing for you, with no obligation.
