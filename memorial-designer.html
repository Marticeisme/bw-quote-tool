Tablet

The stone that stands up and carries the name.

Base

Foundation

The granite slab underneath, level with the lawn.

  • Polished is smooth and shiny. Rock pitch is rough, chipped by hand. Sawn is smooth but not shiny.
  • The standard tablet (P2) is polished on the front and back, with a rough top and sides.
  • Uprights are allowed in Section 6, parts of Section 23 and Cascade Court.

A flat marker

A granite marker set level with the lawn. The top is polished and the sides are sawn. A vase goes in its own 12 by 12 granite block below it.

A bronze marker

Cast bronze with raised letters, mounted on a granite or concrete base that's set level with the lawn. Some sizes have a vase that screws into the bronze. Expressions in Bronze adds a picture from your own photo, etched into the bronze.

What the price includes

The stone, any standard design or emblem, the names, the dates, one line of epitaph, your proof, installation and sales tax.
