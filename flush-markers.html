Overview

What Is a Flush Marker?

A flush marker — also called a flat or lawn-level marker — rests level with the grass rather than standing upright. It creates a clean, unobstructed landscape while providing a permanent, dignified memorial that families can visit for generations.

Flush markers are the standard memorial style at Washington Memorial Park. They are available in granite or bronze, and can serve as a single marker or a companion marker shared between two people.

Washington Memorial Park Marker Policy

Nearly all sections of Washington Memorial Park require flush (flat) markers only. Upright monuments are permitted in very few areas — only Section 6, designated blocks of Section 23, and Cascade Court.

For the vast majority of families, the choice comes down to two materials: granite or bronze. Both produce beautiful, lasting memorials — but granite has become the clear favorite for its unmatched personalization options.

Benches are available in Section 6, 23, designated blocks in 20, and the Rose Urn Garden of Section 18.
Comparison

Granite vs. Bronze: Side by Side

Both materials create dignified, lasting memorials. Here's how they compare on the features families ask about most.

Granite

Natural Stone
  • ✓ Over 20 stone colors available (Cloud White, Absolute Black, Bahama Blue, and more)
  • ✓ Custom engraving — names, dates, epitaphs, emblems, scenes, and religious symbols
  • ✓ Ceramic steel photo portraits in color or B&W (lifetime warranty)
  • ✓ Hand diamond & laser etched portraits carved directly into the stone
  • ✓ Exceptionally durable — resists weathering, staining, and fading for generations
  • ✓ Low maintenance — flush against the grass, easy for grounds crews to maintain
  • ✓ Single & companion sizes from 28×16 up to 36×72 full ledger
Includes granite, engraving, wording (epitaph), and installation. Photos are the only add-on.

Bronze

Cast Metal
  • ✓ Classic, traditional appearance with a warm bronze finish
  • ✓ Raised lettering and standard emblem options
  • — Limited color options — bronze patina only
  • — No photo portraits available on bronze
  • — No hand-etched artwork or custom scenes
  • ✓ Requires a granite or concrete foundation (Barrell Gray standard)
  • ✓ Government bronze markers available for veterans
Bronze markers require a separate granite or concrete foundation, quoted separately.

Memorial Photos

Bringing a Marker to Life with a Photo

Adding a photo to a granite marker transforms it from a simple inscription into a deeply personal tribute. Families consistently tell us that seeing their loved one's face at the gravesite brings comfort in a way that words alone cannot. Here are the three portrait methods available.

🖼️

Ceramic Steel Portraits

Kiln-fired porcelain photo plaques recessed into the marker. Available in full color, sepia, or black & white. The image is fused into the porcelain at high temperature, so it will not fade, peel, or discolor outdoors. Shapes include oval, rectangle, circle, heart, and square.

Starting from $470 (B&W) · $855 (Color) · Lifetime warranty
💎

Hand Diamond Etching

A skilled artisan etches a portrait directly into the granite surface, one dot at a time, using diamond-plated tools. The result is a deep, permanent likeness that becomes part of the stone itself. Best on Absolute Black granite for maximum contrast.

Starting from $2,035 · Absolute Black granite only
✨

Laser Etching

A precision laser creates a detailed image on the granite surface. A more affordable alternative to hand diamond etching, though the impression is shallower. Also available only on Absolute Black granite.

Starting from $1,070 · Absolute Black granite only
