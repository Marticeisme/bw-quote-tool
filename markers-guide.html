Washington Memorial Park Marker Policy

Nearly all sections require flush (flat) markers only. Upright monuments are permitted in very few areas — only Section 6, designated blocks of Section 23, and Cascade Court.

For the vast majority of families, the choice comes down to two materials: granite or bronze. Both produce beautiful, lasting memorials — but granite has become the clear favorite for its unmatched personalization options.

Benches available in Sections 6, 23, designated blocks of 20, and the Rose Urn Garden of Section 18.

SECTION 1

About Flush Markers

The most common memorial style at Washington Memorial Park
A flush granite marker installed at lawn level — the most common memorial style at Washington Memorial Park

A flush marker — also called a flat or lawn-level marker — rests level with the grass rather than standing upright. It creates a clean, unobstructed landscape while providing a permanent, dignified memorial that families can visit for generations.

Flush markers are the standard memorial style at Washington Memorial Park. They are available in granite or bronze, and can serve as a single marker or a companion marker shared between two people.

WHY FAMILIES CHOOSE FLUSH MARKERS
  • Low profile — blends with the landscape
  • Low maintenance — easy for grounds crews
  • Durable — resists weathering for generations
  • Cost-effective compared to upright monuments
  • All-inclusive pricing (stone + engraving + install)
SECTION 2

Why Granite Is the Most Popular Choice

Unmatched personalization that tells your loved one's story
Custom mountain scene, engraving, and companion names — only possible with granite

The number-one reason families choose granite over bronze is personalization. Granite opens up options that simply aren't possible with cast bronze — from full-color photo portraits to hand-carved artwork.

20+ Stone Colors

Choose from over 20 granite colors across three pricing tiers, from Cloud White to Absolute Black.

Photo Portraits

Ceramic steel portraits in full color or B&W — kiln-fired and warrantied for life.

Diamond Etching

A skilled artisan etches a portrait directly into the stone, dot by dot. Permanent and deeply lifelike.

Custom Engraving

Names, dates, epitaphs, religious symbols, emblems, and hand-carved scenes.

Natural Durability

Resists weathering, staining, and UV fading. Keeps its finish for generations.

All-Inclusive Pricing

Every marker includes stone, engraving, wording, and installation. Photos are the only add-on.

SECTION 6

Diamond & Laser Etched Portraits

Permanent artwork carved directly into the granite
A hand diamond-etched portrait on Absolute Black granite — permanent artwork carved directly into the stone

Hand diamond etching is a portrait technique where skilled artisans etch a photograph's likeness directly into the granite, one dot at a time, using diamond-plated tools. The result is a deep, permanent impression — more lifelike and durable than a surface-level laser etch. For best results, the portrait area should be at least 6″ tall, and a high-quality source photo makes a real difference.

Absolute Black granite only. Subject to artistic rendering — not an exact photo duplicate.
