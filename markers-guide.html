SECTION 1

About Flush Markers

The most common memorial style at Washington Memorial Park

A flush marker — also called a flat or lawn-level marker — rests level with the grass rather than standing upright. It creates a clean, unobstructed landscape while providing a permanent, dignified memorial that families can visit for generations.

Flush markers are the standard memorial style at Washington Memorial Park. They are available in granite or bronze, and can serve as a single marker or a companion marker shared between two people.

WHY FAMILIES CHOOSE FLUSH MARKERS
  • Low profile — blends with the landscape
  • Low maintenance — easy for grounds crews
  • Durable — resists weathering for generations
  • Cost-effective compared to upright monuments
  • All-inclusive pricing (stone + engraving + install)

Washington Memorial Park Marker Policy

Nearly all sections require flush (flat) markers only. Upright monuments are permitted in very few areas — only Section 6, designated blocks of Section 23, and Cascade Court.

For the vast majority of families, the choice comes down to two materials: granite or bronze. Both produce beautiful, lasting memorials — but granite has become the clear favorite for its unmatched personalization options.

Benches available in Sections 6, 23, designated blocks of 20, and the Rose Urn Garden of Section 18.

SECTION 7

Diamond & Laser Etched Portraits

Permanent artwork carved directly into the granite
💎

Artisan Hand Diamond Etching

A skilled artisan etches a portrait or scene directly into the granite surface, one dot at a time, using diamond-plated tools. The result is a deep, permanent impression — more lifelike and durable than a surface-level laser etch. For best results, the portrait area should be at least 6″ tall, and a high-quality source photo makes a real difference.

Starting from $2,035 · Absolute Black granite only · Subcontracted specialty work
✨

Laser Etching

A precision laser creates a detailed image on the granite surface. A more affordable alternative to hand diamond etching, though the impression is shallower. Available only on Absolute Black granite for maximum contrast.

Starting from $1,070 · Absolute Black granite only
