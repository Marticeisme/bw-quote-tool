Why Choose a Scattering Garden?

A sacred final resting place with permanent memorialization

A Sacred Final Resting Place — Our gardens at Washington Memorial Park offer a beautifully maintained, consecrated space where your loved one's cremated remains are lovingly returned to the earth — a permanent address to return to.

Permanent Memorialization — Unlike private or remote scattering, our gardens provide an engraved plaque or granite marker — a lasting tribute families can visit, grieve at, and find comfort in for generations to come.

A Place to Return — Families deserve a permanent destination for remembrance — one that is professionally cared for, beautifully landscaped, and always accessible within Washington Memorial Park.

SECTION 5

Understanding Your Package

What every scattering garden package includes
EACH PACKAGE INCLUDES
  • Space Purchase
  • Endowment Care Fund (ECF)
  • Scattering Service Fee
  • Recording Fee
  • Memorial Plaque / Engraving

Scattering Space — Your designated right to have your loved one's cremated remains scattered within the chosen garden. Each space is a permanent, reserved location within Washington Memorial Park's beautifully maintained grounds — a sacred address your family can always return to.

Endowment Care Fund (ECF) — A one-time contribution to the cemetery's perpetual care fund, ensuring the scattering garden and surrounding grounds are professionally maintained, landscaped, and preserved in perpetuity — so the space remains a dignified place of peace for generations to come.

Recording Fee — Covers the official documentation and permanent registration of the interment with Washington Memorial Park. This creates a lasting record of your loved one's final resting place, providing families with certified documentation and peace of mind.

Memorial Plaque / Engraving — A permanent, personalized marker placed within the scattering garden to honor your loved one. Depending on the garden, this may be a granite memorial plaque, a bronze plaque, or an onsite granite engraving — each a lasting tribute families can visit and find comfort in for years to come. Tax (10.4%) applies to this item.

Sales tax (10.4%) applies to memorial plaque/engraving only. All other fees are tax-exempt. Prices subject to change without notice. All fees listed are per interment.
