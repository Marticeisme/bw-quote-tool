SECTION 5WHAT EVERY PACKAGE INCLUDES

Understanding Your Package

What every scattering garden package includes
EACH PACKAGE INCLUDES
  • Space Purchase
  • Endowment Care Fund (ECF)
  • Scattering Service Fee
  • Recording Fee
  • Memorial Plaque / Engraving

Scattering Space. This is your right to have your loved one's cremated remains scattered in the garden you choose. The space is reserved and recorded, so your family always has an address to return to.

Endowment Care Fund (ECF). A one-time payment into the fund that pays for the upkeep of the garden and the grounds around it. It is charged once and it never comes back.

Recording Fee. This registers the scattering with Washington Memorial Park. It is what creates the permanent record of where your loved one was scattered, and it is why we can tell you years later.

Memorial Plaque / Engraving. This is the marker in the garden with your loved one's name on it. Which one you get depends on the garden: a granite memorial plaque, a bronze plaque, or an engraving cut into the granite onsite. Tax (10.4%) applies to this item.

Sales tax (10.4%) applies to memorial plaque/engraving only. All other fees are tax-exempt. Prices subject to change without notice. All fees listed are per interment.

Every package covers the space, the endowment care fund, the scattering service, the recording of it, and the memorial with the name on it. Sales tax (10.4%) applies to the plaque or the engraving only; everything else is tax-exempt. All fees are per interment.
